# Sources and third-party notices

The application code, geometry, material swatches, procedural textures and CS Prefab interface were independently implemented for this project. The current application uses the PrefabPartner SVG logo supplied in the user's workspace. No reference configurator runtime, photo layers or proprietary 3D assets were copied.

- **Poly Haven material maps and lighting:** red brick, wood surface normal/roughness, Kloofendal HDR environment, and (since 2.6) beige plaster (existing house), gravel concrete (terrace tiles), aerial grass (garden) and asphalt (flat roof), distributed under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The original 2.4 files are bundled unmodified; the 2.6 maps are resized to 1024 or 512 px JPEG for page weight, with original and bundled hashes. Asset URLs, hashes and intended uses are in [the provenance manifest](addons/cs_prefab_configurator/static/src/assets/materials/provenance.json). These are representative surfaces, not certified manufacturer samples.
- **Facade finishes derived from two of those scans (since 2.9.4, every brick and wood gevel):** the black, white and yellow brick albedos are **recoloured from the same Poly Haven `red_brick_03` diffuse** the red facade uses, and the wood cladding albedo is the Poly Haven `wood_floor_deck` diffuse **retoned** from varnished floor to oiled timber, both by `scripts/prepare_facade_textures.py` (1024 px JPEG plus a 512 px compact sibling each, +1,516,870 bytes on the full tier and +353,001 on the compact one). No new upstream asset: the four bricks deliberately share one scan's bond, relief and roughness so the finishes a customer compares are one wall in four clay colours rather than four different buildings, and the wood albedo stays pixel-registered with the normal and roughness maps of the scan it came from. The clay is separated from the mortar by the scan's own saturation corroborated with a height map integrated from its normal map, and only the clay is re-mapped along a CIELAB ramp; `docs/verification/2.9/facade-brick-mortar-mask.png` shows the separation. Both derivations are [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license) works of CC0 originals; the upstream URL, the sha256 of the exact source file each was derived from, the recolour description and the measured mean sRGB per file are in the [materials provenance manifest](addons/cs_prefab_configurator/static/src/assets/materials/provenance.json), and `tests/frontend/material_assets.test.mjs` fails if a derived map stops matching the scan that ships. Representative facing bricks and cladding, not supplier samples.
- **Floor finishes (since 2.8, regenerated for 2.9):** both finishes are re-laid by `scripts/prepare_floor_textures.py` from a single oak grain scan, Poly Haven `laminate_floor_02` diffuse/normal/roughness at **2k** ([CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license)) — the plain laminate as 120 × 20 cm boards with wandering joints (1440 px, **2.40 m** tile, 24 boards) and the visgraat as 60 × 15 cm boards in a single herringbone at 45° (1536 px, **3.3941 m** tile, 64 boards), both graded to a warm light "naturel eiken" tone (mean sRGB 213,183,148) and shipped with generated normal, roughness and ambient-occlusion maps plus their own 512 px compact-tier siblings. Every board is cut from a clean band **between** the scan's own plank joints, and both tiles are laid modulo the tile so they wrap exactly; the 45° tile is produced by a resampling warp that maps the irrational rotated period 2L·√2 onto a whole number of pixels instead of rounding it, and rotates the grain normal's XY with it. Scanned herringbone tiles were rejected because their published physical size is unknown and their planks are far smaller than the Dutch visgraat format. Origin URL, hashes, grading, mean tone, board format and tile size per file are in the [materials provenance manifest](addons/cs_prefab_configurator/static/src/assets/materials/provenance.json), and `python scripts/check_floor_tiling.py` re-derives all of it from the shipped pixels. Representative floor finishes, not supplier samples.
- **Poly Haven roof tiles (since 2.9, existing house and neighbours):** `clay_roof_tiles_03` diffuse/normal (1024 px) and roughness (512 px) JPEG maps for the pitched zadeldak roofs, [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license); origin URLs, original and bundled hashes are appended to the same [materials provenance manifest](addons/cs_prefab_configurator/static/src/assets/materials/provenance.json). A representative ceramic tile, not a supplier sample.
- **Poly Haven surface scans and compact ladder (since 2.9, realistic live view):** `white_stucco` normal/roughness/ambient-occlusion maps for the interior stucwerk (the paint colour is the material tint), `concrete_floor_worn_001` diffuse/normal/roughness/ambient-occlusion maps for the screed / kaal-beton floor, and `wood_planks_grey` diffuse/normal/roughness/ambient-occlusion maps for the garden fence, all [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license), prepared at 1024 px JPEG by `scripts/prepare_surface_textures.py` (the screed and fence diffuse maps are brightness graded, recorded per file). The same script derives a 512 px compact-tier ladder (`*_512.jpg`) from every bundled colour, normal and roughness map (brick, house plaster, terrace gravel, grass, roof bitumen, roof tiles, deck wood and the new sets; ambient-occlusion maps have no compact variant). The two floor sets write their own compact siblings in `scripts/prepare_floor_textures.py`, in the same step that writes the canonical maps, so a regenerated floor can never ship with a stale compact variant beside it. Origin URLs, original and bundled hashes, physical tile sizes and the derivation of every compact file are appended to the [materials provenance manifest](addons/cs_prefab_configurator/static/src/assets/materials/provenance.json). Representative surfaces, not supplier samples.
- **ambientCG green-roof scans (since 2.9, sedum roof):** `Moss001` colour/normal/roughness/ambient-occlusion for the sedum mat and `Gravel023` colour/normal for the ballast strip along the roof edge, both [CC0 1.0](https://docs.ambientcg.com/books/website/page/licensing), prepared by `scripts/prepare_green_roof_textures.py`. They replace the procedural cushion texture the roof used before, which read as scattered blobs rather than a planted mat. Neither Poly Haven nor ambientCG publishes a sedum or succulent ground cover (Poly Haven's 861 textures carry no moss ground scan at all; of ambientCG's 49 "moss" hits only `Moss001`–`Moss004` are ground covers, and `Moss002`–`Moss004` are uniform dark cushion moss), so `Moss001` was chosen as the one scan with the grey-green / olive / reddish spread of a sedum mix, colour graded towards it and mapped at 0.90 m instead of its published 0.45 m tile so the rosettes read at sedum scale. The ballast is mapped at 0.55 m instead of its published 1.50 m so the pebbles read at 20–40 mm. Only the sedum colour map is written at 1024 px; every other map is 512 px. Origin URLs, original and bundled hashes, the grade and both tile sizes per file are appended to the [materials provenance manifest](addons/cs_prefab_configurator/static/src/assets/materials/provenance.json). Representative planting and ballast, not a supplier sample.
- **Poly Haven CC0 furniture models (since 2.8, room scenarios):** `sofa_02`, `modern_coffee_table_01`, `ArmChair_01`, `side_table_01`, `potted_plant_04`, `wooden_table_02`, `dining_chair_02`, `GreenChair_01` as glTF with 1k textures resized to 512 px JPEG (`.gltf`/`.bin` unmodified), distributed under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Per-file origin URLs, hashes and sizes are in [the models provenance manifest](addons/cs_prefab_configurator/static/src/assets/models/provenance.json). The furniture is indicative room dressing ("inrichting indicatief, niet inbegrepen"), loaded only when a scenario is selected; beds, wardrobes and rugs are procedural. The glTF loader is the Three.js 0.180.0 `GLTFLoader` (MIT), bundled by `scripts/vendor.mjs` into `static/vendor/gltf-loader.module.js` and imported on first use.
- **Poly Haven CC0 garden set, desk and bookcase (since 2.9, terrace and jeugdkamer):** `outdoor_table_chair_set_01` (folding teak-slat table and chair on an anthracite steel frame — the terrace set, laid out by `preview.js` as one table and three chairs), `SchoolDesk_01` (oak top on a black tubular frame) and `wooden_display_shelves_01` (pine cube shelving with fabric boxes), all [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license), prepared by `scripts/prepare_furniture_models.py`: 1k glTF with the textures re-encoded at 512 px JPEG (quality 86), **+943 kB** (965,412 bytes) over the eight 2.8 models, which takes the models folder from 2,461,048 to 3,426,460 bytes. `outdoor_table_chair_set_01` is the only one whose geometry is not upstream's bytes — Poly Haven publishes it as a styled arrangement with the same chair modelled twice, and the prepare script drops the duplicate node and rebuilds the buffer from the surviving bufferViews (456,856 → 290,280 bytes), recording the upstream sha256 and what was removed in the manifest. The cushions on the garden chairs are procedural (a seat pad and a back pad in outdoor sand canvas): the model ships bare teak and steel, and no CC0 outdoor set with cushions exists. Searched 2026-09-16 across all 521 Poly Haven models: there is no CC0 bed that suits a new extension (only a carved Victorian four-poster, a rusted hospital frame and a floral daybed), no wardrobe at all, and no rug or carpet — those three stay procedural. Origin URLs, upstream and bundled hashes, byte sizes and the trim record per file are in [the models provenance manifest](addons/cs_prefab_configurator/static/src/assets/models/provenance.json), and `tests/frontend/material_assets.test.mjs` re-checks every one of them against the bytes on disk.

- **Three.js 0.180.0:** [upstream](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js), MIT. Included license: `addons/cs_prefab_configurator/static/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`. OrbitControls and HDRLoader import specifiers are rewritten to their locally hosted module; runtime logic is unchanged.
- **Three.js post-processing add-ons (EffectComposer, RenderPass, GTAOPass, OutputPass):** part of Three.js 0.180.0, MIT. Bundled with esbuild into `static/vendor/render-addons.module.js` by `scripts/vendor.mjs`; the shared `three.module.js` stays a single copy. Since 2.9 the realistic look is the live raster view itself — PBR materials, GTAO ambient occlusion, shadow maps and MSAA, no separate render step; the 2.6–2.8 GPU path tracer and denoiser (`three-gpu-pathtracer`, `three-mesh-bvh`) were removed and their licences no longer ship.
- **DM Sans / DM Serif Display:** [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts), SIL Open Font License 1.1. Locally hosted font subsets and OFL notices are included in `static/vendor/`. Browsers make no Google Fonts requests.
- **Inter / Inter Tight:** the current native Odoo website typefaces, distributed under SIL Open Font License 1.1 by [The Inter Project](https://github.com/rsms/inter) and [Inter Tight](https://github.com/rsms/inter-tight). Locally hosted variable WOFF2 files include normal/italic Latin and Latin-ext subsets, including Turkish characters. Full copyright/OFL notices and [download URLs, hashes and subset provenance](addons/cs_prefab_configurator/static/vendor/native-fonts-provenance.json) are bundled beside `native-fonts.css`. Only the native theme bridge loads these definitions; no remote font browser requests are added.
- **DejaVu Sans:** [upstream font project](https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/). The unmodified TrueType font used by the PDF renderer is bundled at `services/fonts/DejaVuSans.ttf`; its full Bitstream Vera license and public-domain DejaVu change notice are in `services/fonts/LICENSE.txt`. PDF generation embeds the font and a Unicode character map, including support verified for Dutch and Turkish names.
- **Playwright and axe-core:** development verification tools only, installed through the locked npm development dependencies. They are not loaded by the product.
- **dart-sass (`sass`):** MIT, a locked npm development dependency. `scripts/preview_website.py` uses it to compile `cs_prefab_website`'s own SCSS so the website pages can be measured in a browser without an Odoo runtime. It ships nothing: on the live instance Odoo compiles the same files with its own Sass.
- **Prefab Partner's own photographs, logos and copy** (`addons/cs_prefab_website/static/src/`): the customer's material, mirrored from prefabpartner.nl and re-encoded to web sizes by `scripts/import_site_media.py`. Every file, its origin and the reason it was resized, recompressed or copied unchanged is recorded in `addons/cs_prefab_website/data/media_index.json`. Not third-party content — listed here so the provenance of everything in the repository is in one place.
